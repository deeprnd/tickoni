# V3.19.S4: Before/After Impact View (IMPACT Function)

**Status:** Backlog
**Epic:** #[github-epic-issue]
**Parent roadmap item:** V3.19: Tickoni Native Investment Terminal
**Labels:** `type/story`, `area/investing`

Show before/after cash, buying power, exposure, and concentration for a
proposed investment action in the IMPACT function panel.

## Product Outcome

Investment users and operators can understand consequence before action: what
cash, exposure, and concentration look like before and after the proposal,
within the IMPACT function panel.

## User Story

As an investment user, I want to see the projected impact of a proposed
investment action, so that I can decide whether the safe action still fits my
mandate and cash constraints.

## Scope

- In scope: before/after cash balance; before/after buying power; sector
  exposure (e.g., technology exposure percentage change); ETF exposure
  percentage; single-name concentration vs. limit; compact alert messages
  (e.g., "cash buffer remains above target", "rebalance not required"); impact
  snapshot identifiers or evidence refs when available; `/show impact` command
  activating the IMPACT function from another panel.
- Out of scope: portfolio optimization, rebalance automation, live account
  aggregation, authoritative balance storage, new financial limits, combined
  investment and payment obligation workspace, thesis health indicators,
  approval expiry countdowns, or pending obligation displays.

## Preconditions And Assumptions

- Impact data comes from governed fixture or `tk_api` response fields.
- The Memory, Analytics, and Ledger roles remain distinct; the UI does not
  connect directly to any store.
- The view renders inside the IMPACT function panel from S1.
- Before/after values are snapshot-based, not live-streaming.
- This story covers investment-only impact; combined investment and payment
  workflows are out of scope per epic.
