# M11: Guarded Money Movement

## Description

M11 proves money movement across payment, bank-transfer, stablecoin, and crypto
transfer workflows:

```text
payment or transfer intent -> policy proposal -> approval-bound movement
```

V11.28 establishes the generic pay-and-move-money guard for payment retries,
payouts, bank transfers, trusted beneficiaries, rails, currencies, countries,
amounts, retry limits, and approval-required proposals. V11.20 extends that
money-movement control surface into guarded crypto transfers with wallet,
network, asset, fee, chain-risk, travel-rule, and sandbox-transfer evidence.

## Included Epics

Product increments:

- `V11.28`: Pay And Move Money Guard
- `V11.280`: Guarded Crypto Transfers

## Why This Is A Milestone

Money movement has higher consequence than recommendation or paper trading.
It deserves a separate milestone because payments, bank transfers, stablecoin
movement, and crypto transfers all need destination allowlists, amount limits,
approval gates, audit records, replay capsules, and no-bypass guarantees before
any execution path is credible.

V11.28 proves the common payment and transfer policy model. V11.280 proves the
crypto-specific dimensions: wallet custody, network selection, fee/gas
coverage, chain-risk scoring, travel-rule evidence, and transfer authority
kept separate from trading authority.

## Completion Signal

M11 is complete when payment, bank-transfer, stablecoin, and crypto transfer
proposals are replayable from deterministic fixtures with allowed, blocked,
evidence-required, escalated, and approval-required flows demonstrated; guarded
crypto transfers can be paper-run or submitted only through explicitly approved
sandbox paths; and no agent or UI path receives direct wallet, bank, payment,
custody, chain RPC, or adapter credentials.

## Summary

Recommendations are cheap. Money movement is consequential. M11 proves that
every path a dollar can take — payment, bank transfer, stablecoin, crypto
transfer — passes through destination allowlists, amount limits, approval
gates, and signed action envelopes with read-back reconciliation. Money
moves only after policy, approval, and audit. No agent or UI path receives
direct wallet, bank, or adapter credentials. The milestone is not "we can
move money." The milestone is "money moves only when everything says yes."
