# M7: Proof And Trust

## Description

M7 builds the proof chain that makes money decisions inspectable and trustworthy:

```text
checkpoints -> tamper-evident capsule -> partner trust surface -> capability catalog
```

Observable hook checkpoints instrument every model call, tool use, proposal,
and approval. A content-addressed, hash-chained replay capsule makes any tamper
detectable at the exact sequence number. Partners can then inspect the audit
timeline, replay the decision without external effects, and understand the
finance-native capability catalog that governed the result.

## Why This Is A Milestone

M2 makes the harness runtime real. M4 makes it inspectable and provable.

It deserves milestone status because it closes a chain where each step is
a prerequisite for the next:

- hooks give field-level before/after state at every governed boundary
- the replay capsule makes that state tamper-evident and self-verifying
- the trust layer exposes the proof to partners without requiring them to
  read raw tile logs or audit JSONL by hand
- the capability surface lets partners understand the complete set of action
  classes, policy outcomes, scope dimensions, and approval paths that
  governed every decision

When M7 is complete, a regulated partner can verify a money-decision record
from content hashes alone, replay it without external effects, and see
exactly why it was allowed, blocked, resized, or approval-required.

## Completion Signal

M7 is complete when a partner can inspect an exported money-decision record,
verify the replay proof bundle from hashes alone, detect a deliberate tamper
with a first-divergence record at the correct sequence number, and browse the
finance-native capability catalog that governed the result.

## Summary

Before M7, Tickoni's audit trail is internal — engineers read JSONL to
understand what happened. After M7, it is partner-facing: a regulated
counterparty can verify a money-decision record from content hashes alone,
detect a deliberate tamper at the exact sequence number, and see why it
was allowed, blocked, resized, or approval-required. The trust chain is:
hook checkpoints → tamper-evident capsule → partner trust surface →
capability catalog. M7 turns audit from a developer artifact into a
trust product.
