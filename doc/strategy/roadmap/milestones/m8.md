# M8: Approved Payment And Broker Sandbox

## Description

M8 proves the payment and broker domain end to end:

```text
guard -> sandbox action -> operations workflows
```

The user can inspect a failed payment, payout, retry, or transfer and produce
an allowed, blocked, evidence-required, escalated, or approval-required
proposal with the same finance-native capability model used for investing.
Approved sandbox connectors then prove that small broker, payment, or bank
actions can pass through signed adapters, signed action envelopes, deterministic
action ids, read-back, reconciliation, and kill-switch controls. Operations
teams can then open non-investment workflows — payment exceptions,
reconciliation breaks, fraud and risk triage, and compliance cases — through
the same governed harness.

## Why This Is A Milestone

M1 establishes investing. M6 establishes payments as a peer domain with the
same safety model.

It deserves milestone status because it spans the full payment arc:

- capability-envelope-governed payment and transfer proposals
- approved sandbox execution through signed adapters and read-back
- operations team access to the same governed harness for non-investing
  workflows

When M8 is complete, the payment domain has the same proposal, approval,
audit, and replay guarantees as the investing domain, and sandbox connectors
prove that small real actions can pass through the signed execution path.

## Completion Signal

M8 is complete when payment and transfer proposals are replayable from
deterministic fixtures with allowed, blocked, resized, and approval-required
flows demonstrated; approved sandbox actions are submitted only through signed,
audited, read-back-verified adapter paths; and operations workflows are
accessible through the governed harness without direct database or adapter
credentials.

## Summary

M1 proved investing. M8 proves payments as a peer domain with the same
safety model: proposal, approval, audit, and replay guarantees. But M8 goes
further — it proves that small real actions can pass through a signed
execution path with read-back and reconciliation, and that non-investment
operations (fraud triage, reconciliation breaks, compliance cases) flow
through the same governed harness. The payment domain is no longer a
secondary concern; it is a full peer to investing.
