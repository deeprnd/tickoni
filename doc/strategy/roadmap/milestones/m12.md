# M12: Social Thesis And Money Feed

## Description

M12 makes money decisions shareable and copyable:

```text
browse -> inspect -> copy into own limits
```

Users can browse thesis cards and money-decision cards published by others,
inspect the risk checks and policy outcomes behind each card, and copy an
investing or payment template into their own account, beneficiary, wallet, and
policy limits. The copied template is re-evaluated against the copying user's
own capability envelope, not the original author's.

## Included Epics

Product increments:

- `V12.30`: Social Thesis And Money Feed

## Why This Is A Milestone

M1–M11 establish safe money decisions across investing, payments, crypto, and
operations, and make them auditable and partner-inspectable. M12 extends the
product surface to social distribution without relaxing any of those safety
properties.

It deserves milestone status because copying a thesis or money-decision
template is a distinct trust boundary: the original author's limits, accounts,
and policy outcomes must not carry over to the copying user. Account-specific
resizing and re-evaluation are the core product work, not the browsing UI.

## Completion Signal

M12 is complete when a copied thesis or money-decision template is
re-evaluated against the copying user's own capability envelope, account
limits, and policy version, and the re-evaluated result is replayable with
deterministic fixtures.

## Summary

A shared investment idea is useful only if it is safe to copy. M12 makes
money decisions shareable — users can browse, inspect, and copy templates
from others — without letting the original author's limits, accounts, or
policy outcomes bleed into the copy. Every template is re-evaluated against
the copying user's own capability envelope. The trust boundary is the copy
operation itself: the original author's permissions don't carry over. Social
distribution doesn't mean social permission — the system re-governs at
the point of copy.
