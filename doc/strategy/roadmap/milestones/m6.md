# M6: Crypto Guard And Spot Trade

## Description

M6 proves the crypto and stablecoin domain end to end:

```text
guard -> spot trade ticket -> trading authority separate from transfer authority
```

Users can check wallet, network, asset, chain-risk, travel-rule, and amount
scope before transfer proposals. A crypto spot-market view becomes a guarded
paper ticket with fee-aware sizing and quote validation. Exchange trading
authority remains distinct from wallet, network, custody, chain-risk, and
transfer authority at every capability boundary.

## Included Epics

Product increments:

- `V6.31`: Crypto And Stablecoin Guard
- `V6.9`: Crypto Thesis To Guarded Spot Trade

## Why This Is A Milestone

Crypto introduces capability dimensions absent from investing and payments:
wallet custody, network selection, chain-risk scoring, travel-rule compliance,
and trading authority that must be separated from transfer authority.

It deserves milestone status because these dimensions require their own
capability envelope shape, destination allowlist, and policy outcome paths.
Proving the crypto domain uses the same finance-native safety model as
investing and payments — without collapsing the distinct authority boundaries —
is the core deliverable.

## Completion Signal

M6 is complete when crypto and stablecoin transfer proposals are
policy-checked and replayable, fee-aware spot trade tickets are governed by
exchange-specific capability envelopes, and trading authority is demonstrably
separate from transfer authority in audit output.

## Summary

Crypto demands capability dimensions that investing and payments don't face:
wallet custody, network selection, chain-risk scoring, and travel-rule
compliance. The instinct is to collapse these into a single permission —
"can trade crypto" — but that is exactly what makes crypto dangerous. M6
proves that crypto can use the same finance-native safety model as investing
and payments *without* collapsing distinct authority boundaries. Trading
authority stays separate from transfer authority at every level. The proof
is the domain; the mechanism is the same.
