# M4: Governance Of Uncertain Futures

## Description

M4 is where Tickoni stops governing what happened and starts governing what
might happen. Before M4, Tickoni governed known actions — paper trades, policy
decisions, audit records. After M4, it governs forward-looking decisions based
on explicit, auditable assumptions: DCF valuations, Monte Carlo uncertainty
bands, sensitivity analysis, and per-share verdicts. Every valuation produces a
`valuation.analysis.propose` capability record with the same policy-gated,
audit-ready, replay-stable treatment as trading proposals. No black-box point
estimates. No opaque spreadsheets. Just transparent assumptions, traceable
computation, and governed output.

```text
company financials -> DCF + Monte Carlo -> proposal-grade valuation
```

The user supplies company financial data and policy inputs, the engine produces a
discounted cash flow (DCF) valuation with base/bull/bear scenarios, sensitivity
analysis, and native Monte Carlo uncertainty bands. Output is a
`valuation.analysis.propose` capability record with explicit scope, evidence, and
replay-stable computation — not a raw number.

## Why This Is A Milestone

M1 proved Tickoni can govern known actions. M2 made those boundaries real. M3
shipped the product. M4 is the qualitative leap: Tickoni now governs *decisions
based on uncertain assumptions*. Prior milestones governed what happened. M4
governs what might happen — with the same audit, replay, and policy enforcement.

It deserves milestone status because it shifts what Tickoni does qualitatively:

- prior milestones governed actions after they were decided; M4 makes Tickoni
  a platform for forward-looking decisions where the inputs are explicit
  assumptions, not just observed facts, and the output is a governed proposal
  not a raw number,
- explicit assumptions replace black-box estimates — ERP, beta, growth rate,
  country risk are auditable and replayable, which no generic valuation tool
  provides,
- uncertainty is modeled, not ignored — native Monte Carlo simulation with
  100,000 iterations on a single core produces base/bull/bear uncertainty
  bands, cross-checks, and sensitivity analysis without Python, R, or any
  external tool boundary,
- valuation enters the capability envelope model (`valuation.analysis.propose`),
  so forward-looking analysis gets the same policy-gated, audit-ready,
  replay-stable treatment as trading or payment proposals.

When M4 is complete, Tickoni can produce a proposal-grade valuation record from
raw financial data that is traceable to input assumptions, reproducible under
deterministic fixtures, and inspectable in CaseOps — with full audit trail and
replay stability, no LLM, no model provider, and no live data feed.

margin of safety, capability proposal, and replay confirmation — all traceable
to input assumptions, all policy-gated, all replay-stable.

The engineer sees the 100,000 native Monte Carlo iterations complete in under
30 seconds on a single core. The compliance reviewer sees the audit trail. The
CaseOps operator sees the proposal card.
