# M4: Invest With Confidence — Governed Forward-Looking Decisions

## Description

Financial decisions are only as defensible as their assumptions. Until M4, Tickoni governed known actions — paper trades, policy decisions, audit records. Things that already happened. You could audit them, replay them, prove what happened.

M4 is the qualitative leap: Tickoni now governs forward-looking decisions with the same rigor. It takes explicit, auditable assumptions — equity risk premium, beta, growth rates, country risk — and produces valuation proposals that are as policy-gated, audit-ready, and replay-stable as any trading or payment action. No black-box point estimates. No opaque spreadsheets. Every valuation comes with the evidence trail to defend it.

```text
company financials -> DCF + Monte Carlo + scenarios -> governed investment proposal
```

The user supplies company financial data and policy inputs. The engine produces a discounted cash flow valuation with base/bull/bear scenarios, sensitivity analysis, native Monte Carlo uncertainty bands, and a per-share verdict. The output is a `valuation.analysis.propose` capability record — not a raw number.

**The reframe:** Generic valuation tools give you a number and hide their assumptions. Tickoni gives you the number AND the ability to prove it. The product is the same — DCF with Monte Carlo — but the meaning is completely different. One is an estimate you might have to defend. The other is a decision you can show to a board, an auditor, or a compliance reviewer and say: "every assumption is visible, every computation is traceable, every result is replayable."

## Why This Is A Milestone

M1 proved Tickoni can govern known actions. M2 made those boundaries real. M3 shipped the product. M4 is the qualitative leap: **Tickoni becomes a platform for forward-looking decisions, not just backward-looking audit.**

Prior milestones governed what happened. M4 governs what *might* happen — with the same audit, replay, and policy enforcement. That shift is not incremental. It is foundational:

- **Trust extends into the future.** Prior milestones governed actions after they were decided. M4 governs the decisions that *precede* action — where inputs are explicit assumptions, not observed facts, and the output is a governed proposal, not a raw number.
- **Assumptions replace black-box estimates.** ERP, beta, growth rate, country risk — every input is auditable and replayable. No generic valuation tool provides this. A number with invisible assumptions is a liability. A number with transparent assumptions is an asset.
- **Uncertainty is modeled, not ignored.** Native Monte Carlo simulation with 100,000 iterations on a single core produces base/bull/bear uncertainty bands, cross-checks, and sensitivity analysis without Python, R, or any external tool boundary. Generic tools either ignore uncertainty or require expensive external tools. Tickoni does it natively.
- **Valuation enters the capability model.** `valuation.analysis.propose` gets the same policy-gated, audit-ready, replay-stable treatment as trading or payment proposals. Forward-looking analysis is no longer a sidebar — it's first-class citizen in the governed runtime.

When M4 is complete, Tickoni can produce a proposal-grade valuation record from raw financial data that is traceable to input assumptions, reproducible under deterministic fixtures, and inspectable in CaseOps — with full audit trail and replay stability, no LLM, no model provider, and no live data feed.
