# M5: See Your Money, In Real Time — Portfolio Visibility And Guidance

## Description

Investing without visibility is gambling with extra steps. Before M5, Tickoni could make proposals and audit them. What it couldn't do was show a user what they actually owned, where their money was concentrated, and what would happen if they changed something.

M5 closes that gap. Users can create and manage portfolios, see their exposure broken down by sector and industry through charts rendered from auditable holding records, receive deterministic rebalance suggestions, maintain watchlists with automatic DCF valuation via V4.32, and select which LLM model powers each investigation. Milestone M5 comprises two epics: V5.26 (Portfolio Management And Valuation Queue) and V5.2 (Model Selection And Governance).

```text
portfolio intent -> policy gate -> auditable exposure -> valuation evidence
```

**The reframe:** Generic portfolio trackers show you where your money is and nothing else. Tickoni shows you where your money is AND how it would behave under policy, under stress, and under valuation. A pie chart is a start. A pie chart rendered from auditable holding records with automatic DCF valuation on the watchlist — that's the difference between a dashboard and a decision platform.

## Why This Is A Milestone

M5 closes the qualitative gap between "Tickoni is a governed event engine with a valuation calculator" and "Tickoni is a consumer investing product with complete auditability." Three prerequisites from M4 converge here: the proven runtime ingestion-normalization-dedupe-audit path, the V4.32 DCF valuation engine, and the tamper-evident replay discipline. M5 wires all three together into a visible loop where a user action (add a holding) produces an auditable event, triggers a valuation, and surfaces a policy-checked proposal. No prior milestone can demonstrate this full loop because none of them owned portfolio state or the valuation queue.

M6 (Approved Payment And Broker Sandbox) depends on M5 because M5 establishes the portfolio state model and rebalance proposal pattern that payment/broker execution later operates on — rebalance suggestions become approved mutations only after M5's proposal surface exists.

This earns milestone status because it crosses from "runtime with a calculator" into "a complete, visible, auditable portfolio lifecycle" — the first consumer-facing increment that proves Tickoni's policy and audit discipline applies to investing, not just to payments and transfers.

It is the moment the product shifts from "can it do the math?" to "can it show me what I own, and what to do about it?"

## Completion Signal

A user can run a deterministic demo with a fixture portfolio (AAPL 50 shares, MSFT 30 shares, TSLA 10 shares, NVDA 20 shares, USD 5,000 cash), see exposure charts render Technology 82% (drift +42%), receive a `portfolio.rebalance.propose` envelope for drift correction, add AMZN to the watchlist, and see AMZN complete a DCF valuation with intrinsic value range and Monte Carlo CI — all replayable from captured inputs with no external side effects.

Integration tests confirm:
- Portfolio CRUD mutations flow through the governed event path and are reconstructable from the audit chain.
- Exposure charts are deterministic functions of auditable holding records.
- Rebalance suggestions are computed by deterministic exposure diff, not agent output.
- Watchlist tickers enter the V4.32 valuation queue with full capability checks.
- Model tier selection (V5.2) is stored as `tkmodl` capability scope and enforced.
- Replay produces byte-identical output with substituted model and adapter outputs.
