# V7.18: Replay Proof Bundle And Evidence Integrity

**Status:** Planned
**Milestone:** M7
**Labels:** `epic`, `area/audit`, `area/platform`, `area/trust`

Build the content-addressed evidence store, hash-chained event references in the replay capsule, agent transcript and tool-call hash references, and expected outcome verification. V1.11 source-driven replay handles substitution and fail-closed behavior; this increment adds the cryptographic integrity layer needed for audit proof bundles and partner-trust evidence.

Extracted from V1.11.S4 (T6–T9).

## Product Intent

V1.11 replay reconstructs the investment flow from captured inputs, routes model calls through `tkmodl` substitution, and fails closed on missing substitutions. What it does not provide is a tamper-evident bundle: evidence items are not content-addressed, the replay capsule does not carry hash references for events, transcript, or tool calls, and there is no expected outcome field to verify replay results against. V7.18 adds this integrity layer so that the capsule is self-verifying and the proof bundle can be shared with auditors and partners without re-running the flow.

## Users And Jobs

- Auditor / partner: verify a replay proof bundle from content hashes and capsule references alone, detecting tamper at the exact sequence number where it occurred.

## Success Metrics

- Identical evidence content produces the same hash regardless of capture time.
- Replay capsule with non-monotonic event-hash sequence is rejected at load, before any replay step runs.
- Tampered captured fixture produces a first-divergence record at the correct sequence number with the first differing field.
- A reconstructed outcome that differs from the expected outcome field causes replay to fail closed with a divergence record.
- An unmodified capsule replays cleanly with a matching outcome.

## Demo Moment

- Command or flow: `just test-integration-tk` with evidence integrity and capsule validation suite passing
- Expected result: same-content hash stability, non-monotonic sequence rejection, tamper detection with first-divergence report, and outcome mismatch fail-closed.

## Scope

### In Scope

- Content-addressed evidence store: canonical content hash, stable item id, source identity, sequence number.
- Hash-chained event references in the replay capsule: ordered event hashes with monotonicity enforcement.
- Agent transcript hash and tool-call hash references in the capsule.
- Expected outcome field for replay verification.
- First-divergence records on tamper or outcome mismatch.
- Integration tests and fixtures for hash stability, sequence validation, tamper detection, and outcome mismatch.

### Out Of Scope

- No changes to replay substitution logic or fail-closed behavior.
- No changes to `tkmodl` or `tktool` dispatch.
- No new product capabilities or policy rules.
- No changes to audit record schema outside the capsule.
