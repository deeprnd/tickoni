# V8.13: Non-Investment Operations Workflows

**Status:** Later
**Milestone:** M8
**Labels:** `epic`, `area/operations`, `area/payments`, `area/audit`, `area/security`, `area/platform`

Open the Tickoni consumer-finance harness to operations teams — payment
exceptions, reconciliation breaks, fraud and risk triage, and compliance case
preparation — through the same governed agent-plus-approval pattern that governs
consumer money decisions, with a CaseOps board as the operator-facing surface.

## Product Intent

V11.28 proved the consumer-facing payment guard: a consumer reviews a failed
payout, Tickoni checks the beneficiary and rail, and proposes a safe retry or
escalation. V8.13 targets the operations tier behind that consumer experience.
A fraud analyst works a velocity spike. A reconciliation analyst resolves a
processor batch mismatch. A compliance officer prepares an AML escalation
packet. All use the same Tickoni harness: event ingested, case created, policy
assigns capabilities, agent investigates through the governed tool broker, audit
records everything, draft-only action proposed, human approves, downstream
response sent only after approval, and replay capsule generated.

CaseOps is the operator UI surface. The approved execution ledger is the authoritative ledger for
balances, transfers, fills, and accounting state — accessible to agents only
through `tkadpt` reads and to approved mutations only through `tkexec` with
policy, signed envelope, and human approval.

## Users And Jobs

- [Payment operations analyst]: Open a payment-exception case in CaseOps, review
  agent investigation findings and evidence, approve or reject draft proposals
  (merchant responses, correction proposals), and view full audit trail and
  replay status.
- [Reconciliation analyst]: Open a reconciliation-break case, review agent findings
  on bank statement vs. ledger mismatches, approve or reject ledger correction
  proposals, and verify read-back reconciliation results.
- [Fraud investigator]: Open a fraud-risk case, review agent investigation on
  velocity spikes or device clusters, approve or reject hold/review proposals,
  and verify no autonomous freezes occur.
- [Compliance officer]: Open a compliance case, review agent findings on AML
  alerts or sanctions matches, approve or reject escalation packets and
  narratives, verify missing-document classification, and prevent autonomous
  regulatory filings.

## Success Metrics

- Every non-investment workflow type (payment-exception, reconciliation-break,
  fraud-risk, compliance) has a deterministic demo command that runs offline.
- Draft operator outputs are non-executing by construction; a bypass attempt is
  denied before any processor, bank, ledger, or compliance action.
- Agents cannot call the approved execution ledger directly; ledger reads are routed through
  `tkadpt` and writes through `tkexec` with approval.
- Replay runs with no live model, processor, bank, risk, compliance, or ledger
  effects; deliberate divergence produces a first-divergence report.
- Every case status transition produces an audit record; no transition is silent.

## Demo Moment

- Command or flow: `just tkrepl-demo` (or exact CaseOps flow) with a
  payment-exception, reconciliation-break, fraud-risk, and compliance fixture set.
- Expected result: Each workflow type ingests a deterministic source event,
  creates a case, runs a bounded agent investigation, produces a draft proposal,
  surfaces it for operator approval, and replays from captured inputs with all
  external effects substituted — no live processor, bank, risk, compliance, or
  ledger calls.

## Scope

### In Scope

- CaseOps case record schema with status enum and append-only transitions, case
  queue and detail views, role-based access control, and deterministic assignment
  and routing.
- Non-investment event sets and normalized schemas for payment exceptions,
  reconciliation breaks, fraud and risk signals, and compliance alerts.
- Operations-level capabilities and capability envelopes: investigate, classify,
  draft proposals, prepare escalation packets — all draft-only, execution via
  `tkexec` with approval.
- Evidence packets for each workflow type with source events, linked records,
  fixture-backed lookups, and policy decisions.
- Policy templates for each workflow type with role-based capabilities, draft-only
  invariant enforcement, and approval gates.
- Draft-only operator outputs: merchant responses, investigator notes, correction
  proposals, hold/review proposals, escalation packets, compliance narratives,
  missing-document classification, and audit packets.
- the approved execution ledger adapter with read-only access through `tkadpt`; ledger
  correction proposals through `tkexec` with human approval and read-back
  reconciliation.
- Deterministic fixtures for each event type: clear, flagged, and edge-case
  examples.
- Replay capsules and deliberate divergence fixtures for each workflow type.

### Out Of Scope

- Autonomous fraud holds or account freezes without approval.
- Autonomous compliance decisions (SAR filing, sanctions action, payout
  approval) without approval.
- Direct approved execution ledger write access from agents, CaseOps UI, or `tkapi`.
- Direct payment, crypto, risk, or compliance adapter execution without the
  approved `tkexec` path.
- Generic CRM or ticketing system replacement.
- The consumer-facing payment-retry flow (that is V11.28).
- The first consumer-money product path.
- Story-level implementation tasks — these are scoped at story creation time.

## Release / Evidence Gate

- What can the user or operator do now that they could not before this epic?
  Operations analysts, fraud investigators, and compliance officers can open cases,
  review agent investigations with evidence, approve or reject draft proposals,
  and view full audit trails — across payment exceptions, reconciliation breaks,
  fraud/risk triage, and compliance workflows.
- What changed from the previous roadmap increment?
  Expanded the governed agent-plus-approval pattern from consumer-facing payments
  (V11.28) to the full operations tier.
- What is this epic's wow-effect: the visible moment in this epic's demo or
  workflow that makes Tickoni feel unlike a generic agent harness?
  An operator sees a case flow from event ingestion through agent investigation,
  draft proposal, human approval, and replay — all offline, deterministic, and
  auditable, across four distinct operational domains.
- How does this epic progress Tickoni's unique value proposition from
  doc/strategy/positioning.md: speed, isolation/control, spend governance,
  policy-gated action, and forensic replay?
  Demonstrates consequence isolation (draft-only outputs), policy-gated action
  via `tkpoly` envelopes, and forensic replay for multi-domain operations.
- Which demo command or CaseOps flow closes the epic?
  Deterministic replay demos for each workflow type, showing full lifecycle
  offline.
- Which account, beneficiary, IBAN, wallet, rail, currency, market, venue,
  asset class, instrument, notional, amount, exposure, and frequency checks are
  enforced, if any?
  Capability envelope checks via `tkpoly` for each workflow type: account,
  destination, rail, venue, market, instrument, amount/notional, frequency,
  holding period, and approval scope as applicable per domain.
- What happens when the requested action exceeds policy, scope, or evidence?
  `tkpoly` returns `deny` or `require_approval`; draft outputs are rejected or
  flagged; no execution proceeds without approval.
- Is execution paper-only, draft-only, sandbox, live, or disabled?
  Draft-only for all operator outputs; execution-class actions (corrections,
  holds) require `tkexec` with human approval.
- Which fixtures, samples, audit records, replay capsules, screenshots, or API
  examples prove the behavior?
  Deterministic fixture sets (clear, flagged, edge-case) per event type; replay
  capsules per workflow type; audit records for all state transitions; divergence
  reports.
- Can replay run without external side effects when replay applies?
  Yes — all model, adapter, processor, bank, risk, compliance, and ledger
  outputs are substituted from captured sources.
- What intentional blocked-flow or divergence example proves fail-closed behavior?
  Direct approved execution ledger call from agent denied; hold proposal without approval denied;
  SAR filing attempt denied; divergence report produced when replayed with changed
  policy or fixture.
