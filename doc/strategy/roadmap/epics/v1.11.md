# V1.11: The Trust Proof

**Status: Next · Milestone: M1**
**Labels:** `epic`, `area/investing`, `area/audit`, `area/security`, `area/trust`, `area/agents`

Turn the useful V1.1 engineering demo into a defensible investment-paper-trade
release. Preserve the deterministic fixture flow, but close the gaps between
direct function tests and the governed Tickoni product path.

---

## Product Intent

V1.1 showed it works. V1.11 proves it can't be gamed.

The offline USD 2,000 fixture demo builds a basket, ticket, paper fill, and
replay-match result. USD 25,000 and restricted SOXL flows are covered by
deterministic tests. Thesis, catalog, basket, portfolio, buy/sell, market/limit,
model, adapter, audit, and replay scaffolding exists. Unit and local-mock
integration lanes are deterministic.

This epic closes the gap between "the demo kind of works" and "the system is
actually trustworthy." Every step runs through governed boundaries, every bypass
is blocked, and the full flow can be replayed to verify results. The demo stops
being an engineering artifact and becomes a trustworthy product experience.

The restriction itself is the product: users don't trust systems they think can
be gamed. Making it impossible to bypass controls isn't just security — it's
a signal that the rules apply to everyone, including the system itself.

Release findings carried from V1.1:

1. Bind paper placement to a validated ticket, exact proposal hash, capability
   envelope, policy decision, account, environment, and deterministic action id.
2. Run the investment demo through governed boundaries: `tkcase`, `tkdisp`,
   `tkagnt`, `tkmodl`, `tktool`, `tkadpt`, `tkaudt`, `tkrepl`.
3. Replace narrow CLI parser with bounded structured intent extraction.
4. Complete the documented V1.1 trade checks with exact denied reasons.
5. Complete ticket and paper-result contracts, including save-as-proposal.
6. Make replay source-driven and fail-closed.
7. Finish `tkmodl` governed boundary for the demo path.
8. Put audit, metrics, diagnostics in the executable demo path.
9. Reconcile roadmap, WBS, integration-test specs, demo commands, release
   status.

## User Story

Same as V1.1, but now every step runs through governed boundaries with full
audit, replay, and no-bypass enforcement.

## Success Demo

One offline command shows not only that the flow works, but that it can't be
broken:

- plain-English AI-infrastructure thesis normalized into bounded intent
- explainable basket and rejected candidates
- affordability and the USD 2,000 allowed ticket and paper fill
- USD 25,000 denial with exact limit and effective maximum
- direct SOXL denial before quote or paper-fill work
- direct-placement bypass denial
- audit, metrics, and diagnostic artifact locations
- replay success from captured substitutions
- deliberate tamper detection with first divergent field and sequence

## Release Gate

- `just test-unit-tk` passes with no unexpected skips
- `just test-integration-tk` passes offline against local mocks
- one deterministic `just test-demo-tk` prints all required scenarios
- explicit live-local-model smoke lane remains opt-in and uses `tkmodl`
- no paper action can bypass validated ticket, policy, capability, action identity
- replay performs no live model, broker, market, trading, payment, or execution
  effects
- all V1.11 release findings have executable evidence or are explicitly de-scoped

## Non-Goals

- no live brokerage order placement
- no margin, options, futures, leveraged or inverse ETF execution
- no production market-data dependency
- no autonomous trading or portfolio rebalancing
- no broad UI, partner API, or sandbox connector expansion beyond the governed
  paper-trade loop
- no supervisor-managed tile processes or Firedancer shared-memory links
  (see platform backlog)
- no per-role or per-case model-call limits or budget-exhaustion terminal states
  (see platform backlog)
- no MCP-compatible tool dispatch normalization (see platform backlog)
- no content-addressed evidence store or replay capsule integrity hashes
  (see platform backlog)

### V1.11.S2: Tkmodl governed boundary

  governed `tkmodl` boundary contract.

- V1.11.S2.T3: Add `TkModlConfig` with provider enablement, model allowlist,
  endpoint mapping, hard timeouts, and hard limits.
- V1.11.S2.T4: Add pure validator with stable decision codes for live allow,
  replay allow, and fail-closed denials.

- Unknown model ids, disabled providers, and oversize requests fail closed.
