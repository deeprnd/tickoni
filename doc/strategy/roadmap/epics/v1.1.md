# V1.1: Investing Copilot — Turn Thesis Into Trade

**Labels:** `type/epic`, `area/investing`
**GitHub Issue:** [6](https://github.com/deeprnd/tickoni/issues/6)

An investor has an idea about the market but no way to check whether it makes sense without risking real money. V1.0 proved that Tickoni can process financial events deterministically. V1.1 gives every investor a personal copilot that reads their plain-English thesis, constructs a real basket of investments, checks whether they can actually afford it, and lets them test the trade in paper mode — with every decision explained, every boundary enforced, and every rejected idea justified. This is not about paper trading. It's about not losing money on bad ideas.

## Product Intent

The problem Tickoni solves here is not that investors lack ideas. They have plenty. The problem is that every investor who has ever bought into a hot thesis and watched it implode knows the cost of testing an idea with real capital. Paper trading tools exist, but they are blank slates — empty portfolios where the investor must already know what to buy. That is the wrong starting point. The logical fix is "build a paper trading simulator." The psycho-logic answer is "start with the idea, not the portfolio." Tickoni's investing copilot inverts the workflow: the investor types what they believe, and Tickoni answers with a concrete, affordable, policy-gated basket, with reasons attached to every inclusion and rejection. The wow moment is not "you can trade without risk" — it's "you can test your actual investment judgment before committing a dollar." The comparison anchor shifts from "a blank brokerage account" to "the cost of one bad idea." Every boundary, every rejection, every affordability check is visible and explainable — because the thing an investor fears most is not losing money, it's losing money for the wrong reasons.

## Users And Jobs

- [Investor]: Type a plain-English market thesis and get a concrete, affordable investment basket with explainable reasoning
- [Investor]: See exactly what Tickoni would reject, why, and what the maximum affordable amount is
- [Operator]: Watch a complete thesis-to-paper-trade flow in a deterministic demo with allowed, blocked, and rejected scenarios
- [Operator]: Replay the entire flow offline with no external dependencies

## Success Metrics

- A plain-English investment thesis (no ticker symbols required) produces a structured, explainable basket in under 10 seconds
- Every instrument in the basket carries an inclusion reason; every rejected instrument carries a rejection reason
- The basket allocation sums to the target notional within rounding tolerance
- Oversized requests are blocked with an exact maximum affordable amount, not a generic error
- The full demo (allowed, blocked, rejected) replays identically from fixtures with no live model, broker, market, or execution calls
- All boundary checks (cash, buying power, notional limits, restricted instruments, venue, sector, holding period, round-trip) return explicit allow/deny/approval-required outcomes

## Demo Moment

- Command or flow: `just demo investing-copilot`
- Expected result: A user types "USD 2,000 in AI infrastructure, no single-name concentration, US ETFs and large-cap equities" and watches it become a basket with four eligible instruments, two rejected instruments with reasons, a trade ticket, an affordability confirmation, and a paper trade execution. The same command then shows the blocked variant: "USD 25,000 for the same basket" is rejected with "maximum affordable amount: USD 3,400" and the exact boundary that triggered the block. No live model, broker, market, or trading calls are made.

## Scope

### In Scope

- Plain-English thesis input with natural-language intent normalization
- Investable universe: US-listed equities and ETFs with restricted-instrument denylist
- Basket construction with concentration rules, sector/theme tagging, and explainability
- Demo brokerage account with cash, buying power, and notional limit computation
- Trade ticket generation with per-instrument line items and guardrail enforcement
- Paper trade execution for allowed tickets only
- Deterministic demo fixtures for multiple thesis scenarios (AI infrastructure, US dividends, cybersecurity, broad market, cash preservation)
- Audit-ready evidence emission for allowed, blocked, and rejected flows

### Out Of Scope

- Live order placement to any broker or exchange
- Margin trading, options, futures, leveraged ETFs, inverse ETFs, or crypto
- Autonomous portfolio rebalancing
- Real-time market data or live pricing
- Multi-account or portfolio management across time
- Human approval workflow for paper trades

## Story Breakdown

### How Many Stories

Six stories. The epic touches ingestion, universe, basket logic, portfolio state, trade execution, and demo — each a distinct capability lifecycle stage that can be independently verified. The decomposition follows lifecycle split (core capability → read surface → state management → policy → replay → demo), with one story per boundary.

### Decomposition Rules

1. **Independent delivery:** each story must produce a verifiable outcome without waiting for every other story.
2. **One capability per story:** ingestion, universe, basket, portfolio, ticket, and demo are separate capabilities, not layers.
3. **Split by capability lifecycle:** S1 core thesis input → S2 universe catalog → S3 basket construction → S4 portfolio and affordability → S5 trade ticket and paper execution → S6 demo, evidence, and closure.
4. **Closure story:** S6 handles evidence, demo, documentation, and quality gates.
5. **Domain boundaries:** each story owns one Tickoni tile's responsibility; no story merges `tknorm` with `tkpoly` with `tkaudt`.

### Bad Splits (Avoid)

- "Thesis input" + "thesis validation" as two stories. Validation is part of the schema contract.
- "Basket construction" + "basket explainability" as two stories. Explainability is part of the basket output contract.
- "Backend" + "frontend" as two stories. Both should be independently testable outcomes.

### Good Split Example

```
V1.1.S1: Thesis Input — ingest plain-English thesis, normalize to structured intent, reject malformed input
V1.1.S2: Investable Universe — instrument catalog with restricted-instrument denylist, theme/sector lookup
V1.1.S3: Basket Construction — generate explainable baskets with concentration rules and allocation weights
V1.1.S4: Portfolio and Affordability — demo brokerage state, cash and buying power computation, max affordable amount
V1.1.S5: Trade Ticket and Paper Execution — convert basket to ticket, enforce guardrails, allow paper-only execution
```
