# V11.28: Pay And Move Money Guard

**Status:** Planned · **Milestone:** M11 · **Labels:** `epic`, `area/payments`, `area/security`

Proves Tickoni governs payment and transfer proposals with the same finance-native
capability model that V5.26 uses for investing, so consumer-finance operators can
inspect, retry, and route money-movement with auditable safety gates.

## Product Intent

V5.26 proved investment intent-to-ticket. V11.28 proves Tickoni is not only an
investing feature — it is a consumer-finance harness that can govern payment
and transfer proposals with a unified safety model. A failed supplier payout,
blocked transfer, or declined authorization becomes an inspectable event with
deterministic classification, beneficiary/rail/amount checks, and an
approval-required path before any money moves.

## Users And Jobs

- Consumer / freelancer / small merchant: inspect a failed payment or payout,
  see why it failed, and propose a safe retry or reroute before money moves.
- CaseOps operator: review payment proposals, approve or deny with evidence,
  and escalate high-risk cases without touching the ledger directly.

## Success Metrics

- A failed supplier payout can be inspected without direct adapter access.
- Missing beneficiary, rail, amount, or currency fails closed with a policy
  denial, not a silent default.
- A safe ACH retry to a known beneficiary returns `allow` as a proposal.
- A retry to an unknown beneficiary returns `deny` before any adapter call.
- A proposal missing required evidence returns `require_more_evidence`.
- A high-risk or sanctions-flagged case returns `escalate` with audit trail.
- Replay of allowed, blocked, approval-required, evidence-required, and
  escalated flows produces stable, hash-chained audit records with no external
  side effects.

## Demo Moment

- Command or flow: `just v10.2-demo` — run a deterministic local replay of a
  failed supplier payout with known-beneficiary retry, unknown-beneficiary
  block, approval-required over threshold, evidence-required, and escalation
  cases.
- Expected result: the demo shows Tickoni classify the failure, propose a safe
  retry with evidence and a draft response, block an unsafe retry, require
  approval when over threshold, and escalate a risky case — all replayable
  offline with audit artifacts and no external calls.

## Scope

### In Scope

- Event fixtures for failed payments and payouts (`payment_failed`,
  `settlement_delayed`, `payout_blocked`, `authorization_declined`,
  `processor_timeout`, `refund_failed`, `capture_failed`).
- Normalization of payment, payout, retry, and transfer intent into recipient,
  beneficiary, IBAN, rail, currency, country, amount, processor, retry count,
  timing, KYC/AML/risk flags, and approval path.
- Deterministic processor and beneficiary fixtures for safe/unsafe testing.
- Beneficiary allowlist and IBAN validation.
- Rail, currency, country, processor, amount, retry-count, retry-timing, and
  beneficiary/day/month limit enforcement.
- Sanctions, KYC, AML, risk, chargeback, and dispute flag checks.
- Capability outcomes: `allow`, `deny`, `require_approval`,
  `require_more_evidence`, `escalate`.
- Draft response schema and evidence packet (source event, processor record,
  beneficiary check, rail/currency check, retry state, risk flags, policy
  decision).
- Evidence hashes attached to retry, transfer, hold, route, and escalation
  proposals.
- Demo flow covering all five outcomes with audit and replay artifacts.

### Out Of Scope

- Autonomous money movement or direct payout approval.
- Ledger posting or approved execution ledger mutations.
- Compliance final decision — Tickoni escalates; it does not adjudicate.
- Generic CaseOps-first UI — evidence is exposed through existing CaseOps.
- Live processor or bank-adapter integration — all inputs are deterministic
  fixtures.

## Release / Evidence Gate

- **What the user can do now:** inspect a failed payment or payout, propose a
  safe retry or reroute, and see whether it is allowed, denied, approval-required,
  evidence-required, or escalated — without moving money.
- **What changed:** V11.1 proved investing; V11.28 extends the capability model
  to payment and transfer proposals with the same fail-closed safety.
- **Wow effect:** an exec can review a failed supplier payout and see Tickoni
  classify it, propose a safe retry with evidence and a draft response, block an
  unsafe retry, and escalate a risky case — all offline and replayable.
- **Unique value:** high-throughput event processing, consequence isolation,
  policy-gated proposals, and forensic replay applied to consumer-finance
  payment flows, not just investing.
- **Demo command:** `just v10.2-demo` — deterministic local replay of all five
  outcomes.
- **Checks enforced:** beneficiary allowlist, IBAN, rail, currency, country,
  amount, retry-count, retry-timing, beneficiary/day/month limits, sanctions,
  KYC, AML, risk, chargeback, dispute flags.
- **Exceeds policy:** returns `deny`, `require_approval`, `require_more_evidence`,
  or `escalate` with user-facing reasons and audit trail.
- **Execution mode:** paper-only / draft-only; no money moves.
- **Proving artifacts:** deterministic fixtures, audit records, replay capsules,
  and demo output for all five outcomes.
- **Replay without side effects:** yes — all external effects are disabled;
  model, adapter, and execution outputs are substituted from fixtures.
- **Fail-closed proof:** a retry to an unknown beneficiary is denied before any
  adapter call; a proposal missing required evidence returns
  `require_more_evidence`.

## Dependencies And Decisions
