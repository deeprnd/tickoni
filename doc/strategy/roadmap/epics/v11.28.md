# V11.28: The Payment Safety Checklist — Deterministic Protection Before Money Moves

**Status:** Planned · **Milestone:** M11 · **Labels:** `epic`, `area/payments`, `area/security`

Every failed payment carries hidden risks: wrong beneficiary, unsupported rail, missing currency, sanctions exposure, retry timing violations, chargeback history. A supplier trying to retry a blocked payout without a complete safety view is gambling, not operating. V11.28 gives operators a deterministic pre-flight checklist — every safety dimension checked, scored, and visible before the first decision is made. An operator doesn't need to validate an IBAN or classify a sanctions flag. They need to know, in one view, whether a payment retry is safe, needs approval, or is dead. Like a pilot's checklist, the machine does the verification; the human makes the call.

## Product Intent

Generic payment tools bolt on compliance checks after the money moves. Tickoni puts the checklist *before* the decision. V11.28 makes `payment_retry.propose` a first-class capability with deterministic pre-flight checks across six dimensions — beneficiary, rail, currency, amount, retry state, and risk flags — so that every proposal arriving at a human operator is already scored, already scoped, and already backed by audit evidence.

The product promise: **an operator sees six safety signals instead of six open questions.** The check is done by the machine. The approval is made by the human. They never share the same risk.

V5.26 proved investment intent-to-ticket. V11.28 proves Tickoni is not only an investing feature — it is a consumer-finance harness that can govern payment and transfer proposals with the same fail-closed safety. A failed supplier payout, blocked transfer, or declined authorization becomes an inspectable event with deterministic classification, beneficiary/rail/amount checks, and an approval-required path before any money moves.

## Users And Jobs

- [Consumer / freelancer / small merchant]: Inspect a failed payment or payout, see why it failed, and propose a safe retry or reroute — with a complete safety view before money moves.
- [CaseOps operator]: Review payment proposals with six deterministic safety signals — green, amber, or red — without needing to understand banking rails or compliance internals.
- [Compliance operator]: Enforce sanctions, KYC, AML, and risk flags as explicit gates, not after-the-fact paperwork.

## Success Metrics

- A failed supplier payout can be inspected and classified without direct adapter access.
- Missing beneficiary, rail, amount, or currency fails closed with a policy denial — no ambiguity, no default-allow.
- A safe ACH retry to a known beneficiary returns `allow` as a proposal.
- A retry to an unknown beneficiary returns `deny` before any adapter call.
- A proposal missing required evidence returns `require_more_evidence`.
- A high-risk or sanctions-flagged case returns `escalate` with audit trail.
- Replay of allowed, blocked, approval-required, evidence-required, and escalated flows produces stable, hash-chained audit records with no external side effects.

## Demo Moment

- Command or flow: `just v10.2-demo` — run a deterministic local replay of a failed supplier payout with known-beneficiary retry, unknown-beneficiary block, approval-required over threshold, evidence-required, and escalation cases.
- Expected result: An operator view showing six safety signals — beneficiary allowlisted ✓, rail supported ✓, currency valid ✓, amount within limits ✓, retry state compliant ✓, risk flags clear ✓ — with a clear policy decision. A second run with an unknown beneficiary shows the exact gate that failed. An over-threshold request surfaces `require_approval`. A missing evidence packet surfaces `require_more_evidence`. A sanctions match surfaces `escalate`. No adapter call to any external system.

## Scope

### In Scope

- Payment event fixtures: `payment_failed`, `settlement_delayed`, `payout_blocked`, `authorization_declined`, `processor_timeout`, `refund_failed`, `capture_failed`.
- Six-dimension safety score: beneficiary allowlist, IBAN validation, rail support, currency/country, retry count and timing, sanctions/KYC/AML/risk/chargeback/dispute flags.
- Capability outcomes: `allow`, `deny`, `require_approval`, `require_more_evidence`, `escalate`.
- Draft response schema and evidence packet (source event, processor record, beneficiary check, rail/currency check, retry state, risk flags, policy decision).
- Evidence hashes attached to retry, transfer, hold, route, and escalation proposals.
- Deterministic processor and beneficiary fixtures for safe/unsafe testing.
- Beneficiary/day/month limit enforcement.
- Demo flow covering all five outcomes with audit and replay artifacts.

### Out Of Scope

- Autonomous money movement or direct payout approval.
- Ledger posting or approved execution ledger mutations.
- Compliance final decision — Tickoni escalates; it does not adjudicate.
- Generic CaseOps-first UI — evidence is exposed through existing CaseOps surfaces.
- Live processor or bank-adapter integration — all inputs are deterministic fixtures.

## Release / Evidence Gate

- **What the user can do now:** inspect a failed payment or payout, propose a safe retry or reroute, and see whether it is allowed, denied, approval-required, evidence-required, or escalated — without moving money.
- **What changed:** V11.1 proved investing; V11.28 extends the capability model to payment and transfer proposals with the same fail-closed safety.
- **Wow effect:** an exec can review a failed supplier payout and see Tickoni classify it, propose a safe retry with evidence and a draft response, block an unsafe retry, and escalate a risky case — all offline and replayable.
- **Unique value:** high-throughput event processing, consequence isolation, policy-gated proposals, and forensic replay applied to consumer-finance payment flows, not just investing.
- **Demo command:** `just v10.2-demo` — deterministic local replay of all five outcomes.
- **Checks enforced:** beneficiary allowlist, IBAN, rail, currency, country, amount, retry-count, retry-timing, beneficiary/day/month limits, sanctions, KYC, AML, risk, chargeback, dispute flags.
- **Exceeds policy:** returns `deny`, `require_approval`, `require_more_evidence`, or `escalate` with user-facing reasons and audit trail.
- **Execution mode:** paper-only / draft-only; no money moves.
- **Proving artifacts:** deterministic fixtures, audit records, replay capsules, and demo output for all five outcomes.
- **Replay without side effects:** yes — all external effects are disabled; model, adapter, and execution outputs are substituted from fixtures.
- **Fail-closed proof:** a retry to an unknown beneficiary is denied before any adapter call; a proposal missing required evidence returns `require_more_evidence`.
