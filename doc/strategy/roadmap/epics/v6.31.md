# V6.31: The Crypto Transfer Checklist — Deterministic Safety Before Human Approval

**Status:** Later
**Milestone:** M6
**Labels:** `epic`, `area/crypto`, `area/security`

Every crypto transfer carries hidden risks: wrong network, unsupported token, unallowlisted wallet, sanctions exposure, travel-rule non-compliance, amount drift. A human operator staring at a transfer request without a complete safety view is gambling, not approving. V6.31 gives operators a deterministic pre-flight checklist — every safety dimension checked, scored, and visible before the first question is asked. An operator doesn't need to know how to validate a wallet or classify a chain risk. They need to know, in one view, whether a transfer is safe, needs approval, or is dead. Like a pilot's checklist, the machine does the verification; the human makes the call.

## Product Intent

Generic compliance tools bolt on a checklist after the fact. Tickoni puts the checklist *before* the question. V6.31 makes `crypto_transfer.propose` a first-class capability with deterministic pre-flight checks across six dimensions — wallet, network, asset, chain-risk tier, travel-rule status, and amount limits — so that every proposal arriving at a human operator is already scored, already scoped, and already backed by audit evidence.

The product promise: **an operator sees six safety signals instead of six open questions.** The check is done by the machine. The approval is made by the human. They never share the same risk.

## Users And Jobs

- [Crypto/stablecoin user]: Know that every withdrawal, deposit, or portfolio move is validated across wallet, network, asset, chain-risk, travel-rule, and amount before it ever reaches a wallet.
- [CaseOps operator]: Review a crypto transfer proposal with six deterministic safety signals — green, amber, or red — without needing to understand blockchain internals or compliance procedure.
- [Compliance operator]: Enforce travel-rule evidence and chain-risk tiers as explicit gates, not after-the-fact paperwork.

## Success Metrics

- A crypto or stablecoin transfer proposal carries six fully populated safety dimensions (wallet, network, asset, chain-risk, travel-rule, amount) before policy evaluation begins.
- Unsupported tokens, unsafe wallets, high-risk chains, and missing travel-rule evidence return `deny` or `require_approval` deterministically — no ambiguity, no default-allow.
- Every allowed proposal, blocked wallet, unsupported network, and missing evidence produces audit and replay artifacts.
- No crypto transfer executes autonomously across paper, sandbox, or live paths.

## Demo Moment

- Command or flow: `just tktest crypto-transfer-proposal --fixture stablecoin_internal_transfer`
- Expected result: An operator view showing six safety signals — wallet allowlisted ✓, network supported ✓, asset confirmed ✓, chain-risk tier L1 ✓, travel-rule compliant ✓, amount within limits ✓ — with a clear policy decision of `allow` or `require_approval`. A second run with an unsupported wallet shows the exact gate that failed. No adapter call to any external system.

## Scope

### In Scope

- Crypto and stablecoin capability: `crypto_transfer.propose`.
- Six-dimension safety score: wallet allowlist, network allowlist, asset allowlist, chain-risk tier, travel-rule status, per-transaction/day/month amount limits.
- Custody account scope and counterparty/exchange account checks.
- Proposal modes: internal transfer, withdrawal, deposit reconciliation, stablecoin cash movement.
- Stablecoin cash-impact view alongside brokerage cash.
- Policy outcomes: `deny`, `require_approval`, `require_more_evidence`, `escalate`.
- Audit and replay artifacts for all proposal outcomes.
- Stub adapters for wallet, network, and asset data (read-only).

### Out Of Scope

- `crypto_transfer.initiate` — denied until approved sandbox/live execution.
- Trading authority or trading execution.
- On-chain transaction submission or signing.
- Real wallet or exchange connectivity; stub adapters only.
- Multi-chain bridge routing or cross-chain transfers.
- AML/KYC/sanctions screening beyond travel-rule status and chain-risk tier.
