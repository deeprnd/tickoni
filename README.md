Markets run continuously. Human attention does not.

Tickoni is an AI investment agent that runs the entire investment operation.

**Traditional financial software stops at information. Tickoni turns intelligence directly into capital allocation.**

Set the capital and risk boundaries. Tickoni researches markets, values companies, invests, and adapts within them.

**Built to research, decide, and deploy capital at machine scale.**

```text
market research
→ investment thesis
→ valuation
→ portfolio risk management
→ invest
→ monitor
→ adapt
→ repeat
```

## ⚡ One AI Agent. No Handoffs.

What once moved between research tools, analysts, portfolio managers, and execution systems now runs as one uninterrupted investment loop.

Tickoni runs the complete investment loop continuously:

- Independently researches markets and generates investment theses
- Values companies using industry-specific methods and benchmarks
- Finds underpriced opportunities across growth and value
- Builds and manages a focused portfolio within capital and risk limits
- Turns every decision and outcome into institutional memory

## 🧠 Built to Compound Judgment

Most investment software starts each session from zero. Tickoni compounds institutional memory across decisions.

- Preserves the reasoning behind every investment decision
- Compares theses and valuations with actual outcomes
- Recognizes patterns across decisions and market conditions
- Identifies recurring weaknesses across decisions
- Carries relevant evidence into the next decision

## Core principle

> **You define the capital and the mandate. Tickoni runs the operation.**

## 🛡️ Autonomous, but bounded

Tickoni makes autonomous investment decisions within system-enforced portfolio, cash, approval, and execution controls.

**Tickoni cannot:**

- Exceed position or portfolio exposure limits
- Spend protected cash reserves
- Bypass required approvals
- Execute through unapproved brokers or channels
- Change or override its own mandate

These boundaries are enforced by architecture, not prompts.

**AI can hallucinate. Tickoni won’t let it YOLO your portfolio.**

No 3 a.m. meme-stock spree. No mystery broker. No blank cheque.

## Systems foundation

Tickoni’s systems foundation originates in Firedancer, Jump Crypto’s high-performance Solana systems codebase, shaped by low-latency trading engineering.

Firedancer contributes low-level C infrastructure for high-throughput networking, preallocated memory workspaces, concurrent processing, process isolation, and restrictive sandboxing.

Tickoni turns that systems foundation into an autonomous investment operation—research, valuation, portfolio construction, controlled execution, and institutional memory.

The result is a system designed to keep intelligence, authority, and execution separated.

## Status

Tickoni is experimental. Start with paper capital, conservative limits, and staged approvals before allowing live execution.
